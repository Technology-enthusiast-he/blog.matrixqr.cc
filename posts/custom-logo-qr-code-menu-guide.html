Enhance Brand Trust: How to Add Your Custom Logo to QR Code Menus

Generic black-and-white QR codes are easily ignored. Learn how MatrixQR's new custom logo feature helps restaurants build trust, improve scan rates, and maintain brand identity.

Why Branding Your QR Code Menu Matters

In today's dining environment, security and customer perception are paramount. Unbranded, plain QR codes can make customers hesitate due to security concerns like QR-phishing. By embedding your restaurant or cafe logo directly into the center of the QR code, you provide instant brand verification that reassures diners before they scan.

What’s New in MatrixQR Menu Generator

The latest release at menu.matrixqr.cc introduces full support for custom branding overlay without sacrificing scannability:

  • Instant Logo Upload: Drag & drop your brand's PNG, JPG, or SVG logo file directly in the browser.
  • Error Correction Protection: Automatically increases error correction levels to ensure the code remains 100% readable even with a logo center block.
  • Zero Server Storage: Your logo image is processed entirely inside your local browser canvas. MatrixQR never stores or logs your graphics.
  • High-Resolution Export: Download print-ready QR codes with integrated logos perfect for acrylic table stands, stickers, or printed table mats.

Step-by-Step: Adding Your Logo in Seconds

  1. Go to menu.matrixqr.cc.
  2. Upload or link your existing menu image.
  3. Under the branding section, select Upload Custom Logo and select your logo file.
  4. Preview the scannability on screen and click Download High-Res QR.
