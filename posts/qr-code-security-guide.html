Why "Client-Side" Matters for Security

Most free QR generators process your data on their servers. When you type in your home WiFi password, that password travels over the internet, gets processed on a remote machine, and then a QR code is sent back. While often encrypted, this introduces unnecessary risk.

MatrixQR is different. We utilize modern JavaScript libraries to generate QR codes locally on your device. Your data never leaves your computer. This makes MatrixQR the ideal choice for:

  • WiFi Sharing: Keep your network credentials 100% private.
  • Business Cards (vCard): Protect personal phone numbers and emails.
  • Offline Use: Once loaded, MatrixQR works without an internet connection.
