Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

At MatrixQR Studio (accessible via matrixqr.cc and blog.matrixqr.cc), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded, and how we use it.

🔒 100% Client-Side Processing

MatrixQR is engineered as a zero-backend, client-side web application. All QR code generation, vCard encoding, and custom parameters are processed locally inside your browser. We never transmit, store, or analyze your input data or generated QR contents on remote servers.

1. Log Files & Analytics

MatrixQR follows standard procedures of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, MatrixQR uses basic local storage or session storage solely to preserve user interface preferences (such as selected language or dark mode state). We do not use persistent tracking cookies for cross-site behavioral profiling.

3. Third-Party Links & Hosting

Our website is hosted on Vercel and GitHub Infrastructure. Third-party content delivery networks (CDNs such as jsDelivr and Unpkg) may gather technical headers necessary to deliver static assets efficiently. Please consult the respective Privacy Policies of these infrastructure providers for more detailed information.

4. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
