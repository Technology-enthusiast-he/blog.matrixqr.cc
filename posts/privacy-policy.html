Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

At MatrixQR Studio (accessible via www.matrixqr.cc, blog.matrixqr.cc, and menu.matrixqr.cc), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded, and how we use it.

🔒 Client-Side Processing & Secure Media Hosting

For supported QR-generation workflows, the QR payload can be encoded in your browser without sending the entered content to a dedicated QR-generation endpoint. This describes the payload-encoding step only: the page may still load analytics, scripts, fonts, or other remote assets, and hosting or CDN providers may process ordinary visit requests. For our Digital Menu Service (menu.matrixqr.cc), uploaded menu images or PDFs are stored in cloud object storage so they can be served through public URLs encoded in QR codes.

1. Log Files & Analytics

The websites may produce ordinary server, hosting, CDN, or security logs. Depending on the provider and configuration, these records can include IP addresses, browser or user-agent details, request timestamps, requested URLs, referrers, and related technical information. Do not assume these records are anonymous merely because QR payload generation runs in the browser; handling and retention depend on the relevant provider and configuration.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

Some interface preferences may be stored in browser local or session storage. In addition, the current page code includes a Google Analytics tag that is loaded after a user interaction or after a short delay. The implementation visible in this page does not gate that load on a consent choice. Google Analytics may process page-visit information and may use cookies or similar technologies depending on configuration and browser behavior. Therefore, this page should not be described as having no analytics or no tracking technologies. Review the analytics configuration and applicable consent requirements before relying on this policy.

3. Digital Menu Image Hosting & Cloud Storage

When using menu.matrixqr.cc to create menu QR codes, you may upload pre-designed menu files (such as JPG, PNG, WEBP, or PDF).

  • Storage Provider: Uploaded menu files are hosted on encrypted, high-availability cloud object storage (Cloudflare R2 Infrastructure).
  • Purpose: These files are stored solely to provide a public URL accessible via customer QR code scans.
  • Processing scope: In the described workflow, logo overlays and styling adjustments can be rendered in the browser Canvas. This does not mean the uploaded source file remains local: the menu file itself is uploaded for hosting. The statements here describe the intended use of the file and should be kept aligned with actual service operations and provider retention settings.

4. Third-Party Links & Hosting

Our websites are hosted on Vercel, Cloudflare, and GitHub Infrastructure. Third-party content delivery networks (CDNs such as jsDelivr and Unpkg) may gather technical headers necessary to deliver static assets efficiently. Please consult the respective Privacy Policies of these infrastructure providers for more detailed information.

5. Consent

This policy describes the site's stated practices; it does not replace any consent or notice that may be required for analytics or other processing in a user's jurisdiction. Where consent is required, the implementation should obtain it before loading the relevant technology.
