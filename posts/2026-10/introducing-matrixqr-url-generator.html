The Hidden Risk of Traditional Online Link Generators

Some cloud-based free link QR generators process entered URLs on their servers. Before using one, check whether the QR payload is uploaded, whether the code uses an intermediate redirect, and what data the provider logs:

  • Payload handling and page logs: Depending on the provider, an entered URL may be sent to a remote service for generation. Hosting and analytics systems may also process page-visit metadata; check the provider's privacy disclosures and network behavior.
  • Intermediate redirects: Some dynamic QR services route scans through a provider-managed URL so the destination can be changed or scans measured. If that redirect service becomes unavailable, the code may stop reaching its destination; review the provider's terms and service continuity.

How MatrixQR Processes URL QR Codes in Your Browser

MatrixQR uses local JavaScript execution for the URL-to-QR encoding step. This means the entered URL can be converted in the browser without sending the QR payload to a generation API; it is not a guarantee that all page-related network activity or analytics is absent:

  1. Local Strings Processing: Your website address is converted into a binary barcode matrix directly in your browser JavaScript thread.
  2. Direct Link Encoding: The generated matrix encodes your exact target URL (with automatic protocol validation for http:// or https://), ensuring scanners land directly on your site with zero intermediate redirects.
  3. Browser-side payload processing: URL encoding and QR rendering take place in the browser rather than through a QR-generation API. The page may still load external assets or analytics, and hosting providers may log page requests.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does MatrixQR track clicks or redirect my URL QR code?

MatrixQR generates direct static URL QR codes that do not use a MatrixQR redirect endpoint or built-in scan analytics. The destination website may still log visits or use its own analytics.

Will my generated link QR code ever expire?

A static QR code has no built-in expiry mechanism, but the destination URL can stop working or change. The code itself also needs to remain readable.

Is my website address sent to any external server during generation?

The URL-to-QR encoding step runs in the browser and does not require sending the entered URL to a QR-generation API. The page may still request external assets or analytics, and hosting providers may log page visits.

Can I add my company logo to the website link QR code for free?

MatrixQR's generator supports logo and matrix-color customization as described in this guide. Check the current generator interface for available options and any applicable terms.
